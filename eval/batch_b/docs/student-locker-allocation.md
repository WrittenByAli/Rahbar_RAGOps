# Student Locker Allocation Procedure

## Applying for a locker

Lockers are allotted for one semester at a time. Applications open on the first
day of classes and stay open for ten days. A student may hold only one locker.
When there are more applications than lockers, those registered in two or more
lab courses are served first, and the remaining lockers are given out by draw.

## Charges

The locker charge is Rs 1,200 per semester. A key deposit of Rs 700 is paid with
the first allotment and is returned when the key is handed back and the locker
is found undamaged. A student who keeps the same locker for the next semester
does not pay the deposit again.

## Lost keys and damage

Report a lost key to the Administration Office. The replacement charge is
Rs 450, and the lock is changed within two working days. Staff open a locker
without the key only when the registered holder is present with an identity
card. The cost of repairing damage caused by the holder is taken from the
deposit first.

## What may not be stored

Do not store perishable food, chemicals, loose lithium battery packs, sharp
tools or cash. The university is not responsible for the loss of anything left
in a locker. The Administration Office may inspect a locker, in the presence of
the holder where possible, if there is a safety concern.

## Vacating a locker

Lockers must be emptied within seven days after the last final examination.
Anything still inside after that is removed, listed and held for 30 days. Items
not collected within those 30 days are disposed of, and the key deposit is
forfeited.
