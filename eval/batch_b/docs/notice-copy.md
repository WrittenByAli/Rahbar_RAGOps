This is a fictional document written for coursework testing, and it is not an
official FAST-NUCES publication.

Printing in the computing labs works on a page allowance. Each registered student
receives 150 free black and white pages in a semester. The allowance belongs to
one student, cannot be shared or transferred, and whatever is left unused lapses
when the semester closes.

Pages beyond the allowance are charged to the student's print account at Rs 6
for each black and white page and Rs 25 for each colour page. Colour pages are
never taken from the free allowance. The print account is topped up in advance at
the accounts counter, and a job is not released if the balance is too low.

A print job waits in the queue until the student releases it at the printer with
an identity card. Jobs that have not been released within 24 hours are deleted,
and no pages are charged for them.

A single job may not be longer than 40 pages. Lecture slides should be printed at
least two to a page. Printing complete books, or material that has nothing to do
with studies, is not allowed.

If a page is spoiled by a printer fault, show it to the lab attendant on the same
day. The attendant returns the page to the allowance or credits the print
account. Pages spoiled because of a wrong print setting chosen by the student are
not returned.

Repeated misuse of the lab printers can lead to printing being blocked for the
rest of the semester.
