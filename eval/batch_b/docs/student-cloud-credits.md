# Cloud Computing Credits for Student Projects

Fictional coursework test document. Not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

The department shares a limited pool of public cloud credit among student
projects. This page says who can get credit, how much, and what happens when it
runs low.

## Allocations

A final year project group may receive up to USD 120 of credit in a semester.
For a taught course that needs cloud resources, each enrolled student may
receive up to USD 25 for that course. Credit is not paid out as cash and cannot
be moved from one group to another.

## How to request credit

The course instructor or the project supervisor files the request, not the
student. The request names the project code, the services needed and an
estimate of monthly use. The Cloud Lab Coordinator processes a complete request
within four working days.

## Spending alerts and suspension

Every project account has automatic alerts. The group and the supervisor are
warned when 50 percent of the allocation has been used and again at 80 percent.
At 100 percent the account is suspended and running resources are stopped. A
supervisor may ask for one top-up of at most USD 40 in a semester, with a short
explanation of why the first allocation was not enough.

## GPU instances

GPU instances need written approval from the supervisor before they are started.
A group may use at most 20 GPU hours in a calendar month. A GPU instance that
has been idle for 60 minutes is shut down automatically.

## Rules of use

- Tag every resource with the project code. Untagged resources may be deleted
  without notice.
- Cryptocurrency mining and public file hosting are not allowed.
- Do not place real personal data about students or staff on project accounts.
- Never commit access keys to a source code repository. Report a leaked key to
  the Cloud Lab Coordinator on the day you discover it.

## End of semester

Unused credit does not carry forward to the next semester. Download your work
and delete your resources within ten days after the semester ends. After that
the project account is closed and anything left in it is removed.
