Makerspace Equipment Loan Rules

The makerspace lends electronics and prototyping equipment to students for course work and projects. These rules cover borrowing, returning and the use of the 3D printers.

Safety induction

Before borrowing anything, a registered student completes a two-hour safety induction. The induction stays valid for 12 months and must then be taken again.

Loan periods and late charges

A student may have no more than three items on loan at the same time.

EquipmentLoan periodLate charge per item per day
Development boards and sensor kits14 calendar daysRs 150
Digital multimeters and logic analysers7 calendar daysRs 200
Portable oscilloscopes and soldering stations3 calendar daysRs 400

The total late charge for one item is capped at Rs 3,000. A loan may be renewed once, for seven days, if no other student is waiting for the same item. Borrowing is suspended while any item is more than 21 days overdue.

Collecting and returning equipment

Equipment is issued from the makerspace counter between 9:00 and 16:00 on working days. The student checks the item with the lab attendant at the time of issue and again on return. A fault that is noticed during use is reported at once; the student must not try to repair the item. If an item is lost or damaged through careless use, the student pays the cost of repair or of a replacement of the same specification.

3D printer booking

A printer is booked for at most six hours in one slot, and a student may hold one future booking at a time. Filament is charged at Rs 12 per gram of finished print, including support material. A print that fails because of a printer fault is repeated without charge. A booking that is not started within 20 minutes of the slot time is released to the next student.

Items that stay in the lab

Bench power supplies, the laser cutter and the 3D printers are never lent out and are used only inside the makerspace under staff supervision.
