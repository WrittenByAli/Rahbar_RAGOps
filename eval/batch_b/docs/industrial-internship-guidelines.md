# Industrial Internship Guidelines for Undergraduate Students

## 1. Who can apply

An undergraduate student may start an industrial internship after completing at
least four regular semesters and at least 66 credit hours. A student whose
registration is suspended cannot start a placement.

## 2. Duration and type of placement

An internship lasts at least six consecutive weeks and at least 180 working
hours. Two shorter placements cannot be added together to reach these figures.
Paid and unpaid placements are both accepted. A fully remote placement is
accepted only if the company supervisor holds at least two live meetings with
the intern every week. A placement in a business owned by a member of the
student's immediate family is not accepted.

## 3. Approval before starting

The student submits an internship request to the departmental Internship
Coordinator at least 15 working days before the proposed start. The request
includes the offer letter on company letterhead and a one-page work plan. The
Coordinator replies within seven working days. A placement that begins without
approval is not recorded.

## 4. During the internship

The student keeps a weekly logbook, and the company supervisor countersigns each
weekly entry. Absence of more than three working days in total must be made up
before the placement is counted as complete. A change of host company needs a
new request.

## 5. Report and assessment

Within the first two weeks of the following semester, the student submits the
signed logbook, the company supervisor's evaluation form and a report of 2,000
to 3,000 words. The student then gives a ten-minute presentation to a panel of
two faculty members.

The combined score is made up as follows: company supervisor's evaluation 40
percent, written report 35 percent and presentation 25 percent. A combined score
of at least 60 percent is needed to pass.

## 6. Entry on the student record

The result is entered on the internship record as Completed or Not Completed.
The internship carries no credit hours and does not change the SGPA or the CGPA.
A student marked Not Completed may resubmit the report once within 30 days of
the result. A second unsuccessful submission requires a new placement.
